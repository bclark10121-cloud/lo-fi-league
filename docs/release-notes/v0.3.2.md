# Lo-Fi League v0.3.2

A browser crash fix. On the Retroid Pocket Flip 2 browser, v0.3.1 stopped on the Godot logo with "memory access out of bounds". Nothing else changed.

## Five minute test

1. On the Retroid, open the game page in the browser and press Play in the browser. The title card shows Version 0.3.2.
2. Close the tab. Open the game again. Do this three times. It reaches the title card every time.
3. Tap once on the game. The title music starts.
4. Start an exhibition match. Play a minute. The whistle, kicks and crowd come through over the music.
5. Install the APK over v0.3.1. It opens and plays as before.

## New in this version

- The Sfx sound channel now routes to Master. v0.3.1 moved the Music and Sfx channels into a sound layout file, but the Sfx channel had no route. While Godot loads that file in a browser, it looks up each channel's route before the channel exists. With no route, the lookup ran past the end of its list into unset memory. Sometimes that memory looked harmless and sometimes it crashed. That is why the desktop loaded and the Retroid did not.
- The same missing route left sound effects silent in the browser in Tiny Titans Football and Tiny Tailwind before their fixes. This fix routes Lo-Fi League's sound effects too.
- The engine moved from Godot 4.6.1 to Godot 4.6.3, the newest 4.6 patch. 4.6.3 has the same sound layout code, so the route is the real fix.
- The sound test now fails if any channel has no route.

## Install

Version 0.3.2 installs over 0.3.1 and keeps your saves and settings.

## Full test checklist

1. Browser on the Retroid: the game reaches the title card on every load.
2. Browser: the title music plays after the first click. The whistle, kicks and crowd play during a match.
3. Browser: Music and Sound in Settings still change their level. Sound Off silences the effects and leaves the music.
4. Android: the APK installs over v0.3.1, opens and plays with sound.

## Not tested

- The Retroid itself: the crash never happened on the test machine, old build or new (12 loads each). Your Retroid test is the real proof.
- Real devices: no phone, tablet or real Xbox controller was tried.
- Sound was measured in headless Chrome by a script that reads the page's audio output. Nobody listened by ear. The script hears the page as a whole, not the sound effects on their own.
