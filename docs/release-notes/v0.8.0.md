# Lo-Fi League v0.8.0

Lo-Fi League turns arcade. The ball moves up the field faster, goals come often and defenders sit off. A new super shot fills up as you play well. Every player is now a real 3D low-poly model in his club's colors.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.7.3. Your saves, records and league stay.
2. Start an exhibition. The players are the new low-poly models in both clubs' kits, each with his own skin and hair.
3. Play two minutes. Passes zip, players run faster and restarts come quickly. Expect goals both ways.
4. Watch the SUPER bar under the score. Passes that arrive, through balls, skill moves and won tackles fill it.
5. When it reads SUPER READY, hold Shoot all the way near the goal. The ball glows, leaves a trail and nobody blocks it. A goal shows SUPER GOAL.

## New

- New players: a retro low-poly 3D model made in Meshy from Gemini art (BC's pick C, one body). The game paints each club's shirt, trim, shorts and socks over it, plus each player's own skin and hair. Players run, walk and stand.
- Super shot: the SUPER meter fills from good play. Full, a fully charged shot becomes a super shot. Defenders cannot block it and the keeper stops about 3 in 10. The computer uses it too.

## Changed (arcade, both sides)

- Faster: players run about 15 percent faster, passes are harder and the ball rolls further.
- Quicker restarts: kickoffs, throw-ins, corners and goal kicks start sooner, and play returns to kickoff sooner after a goal.
- Easier scoring: a wider and taller goal, keepers react later and dive slower, and shots stay on target more.
- Looser defense: every side jockeys and lunges less and wins fewer tackles, the computer and your teammates alike.
- The computer shoots more often and from further out.
- How to play explains the super shot.

## Judgment calls

1. The chill defense now covers every side in every match, bot runs too, as you picked both sides.
2. The super meter fills about once or twice a side each match.
3. Meshy's library idle would not attach, so a standing player holds the walk at its mid-step frame. No credits spent on it.
4. Meshy Image Enhancement was off so the marker colors stay exact. The model is remeshed to 9,422 faces.
5. The model file is slimmed from 11.3 MB to 2.3 MB (color map at 1024, no normal or roughness maps). The flat low-poly look does not use them.
6. The version had drifted (0.7.2 in the code, 0.7.3 in the build files). All now read 0.8.0, code 19.
