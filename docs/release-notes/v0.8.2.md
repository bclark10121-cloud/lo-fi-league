# Lo-Fi League v0.8.2

Two of your picks from the research page: a reward card after every match and star players. The start screen title also gets a clear gap between Lo-Fi and League.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.8.1. Your saves stay.
2. The start screen shows Lo-Fi and League with a clear gap.
3. Start an exhibition. One player on each side has a spinning gold diamond overhead. That is the club's star. Take control of yours and a line shows his name and skill.
4. Finish the match, or use Tester tools, Full time now. Pick one of three cards. It turns over to XP or a new Locker look, and the other two show what you missed.

## New

- Reward cards (picked by BC, 2026-11-21): after every exhibition and league match, three face-down cards. Each is XP or a Locker look you do not own yet. A win gives better odds (looks: 30 percent a card on a win, 20 on a draw, 12 on a loss). Not after practice.
- Star players (picked by BC): one captain per club with a gold diamond and a skill. Curve shot bends to the far corner and the keeper reacts late. Rocket shot flies about 12 percent faster. Speed burst sprints 15 percent faster and tires slower. Magic feet does skill moves twice as often and every close defender buys the feint. The wall wins tackles far more often. The computer's star uses his skill too. How to play lists them.
