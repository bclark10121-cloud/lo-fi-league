# Lo-Fi League v0.6.2

A new, sharper app icon: a soccer ball wearing headphones, built as a real 3D model.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.6.1. Your saves stay.
2. Find Lo-Fi League on your home screen. The icon is a 3D ball with cream headphones, mint ear cups and a coral pad, on a sunset sky over a striped pitch.
3. Long press the icon or turn on themed icons. The one-color version shows a ball wearing headphones.
4. Open the game page in your browser. The icon at the top and in the browser tab is the new one.
5. Open the game and play a few minutes. Nothing else changed.

## New in this version

- A new icon. The ball has the classic black pentagons and white hexagons with thin seams. The headphones have soft light and shadows. You picked Mint from three colors.
- The same icon on Android, the game page and the browser tab.
