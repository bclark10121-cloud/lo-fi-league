# Lo-Fi League v0.7.0

Play holding your phone upright or sideways. Back now sits at the top left of every menu screen, clear of Android's bottom bar.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.6.2. Your saves stay.
2. Hold the phone upright and open the game. The start screen fits the tall screen.
3. Tap Settings. Back sits at the top left. The list scrolls under it. Tap Back.
4. Tap Play, pick a slot, then League. Each screen shows Back at the top left. The clubs stand in one column.
5. Start a match upright. The camera looks down from higher up and shows both sidelines. Turn the phone sideways mid-play. The match keeps going and the view switches.
6. Open the Menu, then How to play. Back sits at the top left and the text scrolls.

## New in this version

- Full play upright and sideways, on Android and in the browser. The game turns with your phone.
- Upright: a steeper camera that shows both sidelines, clubs in one column, the league info under the table and the Menu row under the scoreboard.
- Back at the top left of Settings, the slot list, the modes, the club picker, the league table and How to play. No Back button sits along the bottom edge.
- With a controller, up from the first row reaches Back.
- Turning the phone mid-match keeps the match. Nothing reloads.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.13.0: the shared header bar with Back at the top and the screen fit for both orientations.

## Install

Version 0.7.0 installs over 0.6.2 and keeps your saves. The game follows your phone's rotation lock. Turn the lock off to play upright.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine: the new five-size browser check (phone and tablet both ways plus desktop) passed every step, including a turn mid-match. The controller, browser, comfort, review, Tester tools, sound, flow, rules, bot match and bot season checks passed. The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate, no internet permission and rotation that follows the device.

Not tested: a real phone, a real tablet or the Retroid. The Settings browser test is unreliable on the test machine. It failed on different steps across runs and also failed on v0.6.2, so it was not counted.
