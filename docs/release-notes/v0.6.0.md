# Lo-Fi League v0.6.0 "Flow"

The beautiful game, the chill way. Skill moves, through balls, teammates who run into space and a calm defense that gives you room to play. More arcade, less realism.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.5.0. Your saves, records and achievements stay. Music starts as soon as you press anything on the start screen.
2. Start an Exhibition with any club. Win the ball and run at a defender.
3. Press Skill (X on an Xbox controller, I on a keyboard, Skill on touch):
   - No stick: a Roulette. Your player spins away from the defender.
   - Stick forward: a Flick. The ball pops up over the defender's foot.
   - Stick sideways: a Step-over. A quick cut to that side.
   The name of the move shows for a moment. A tackle during the move never wins the ball.
4. Watch your forwards. When your side has the ball they sprint in behind the last defender.
5. Aim at a runner and press Through (Y, L or Thru). The ball rolls into the space ahead of them and you take over the runner. Keep running onto it and shoot.
6. Notice the defense. Opponents stay a couple of yards off you and tackle less often. Short passes feel quicker.

## New in this version

- Skill move button with three moves: Flick, Step-over and Roulette. Close defenders often buy the feint and stumble.
- Through ball button. It picks the best runner in the direction you aim and leads them into space.
- Forward runs. Up to two forwards or attacking midfielders run in behind the defense whenever your side has the ball.
- Chill defense. The computer side jockeys instead of charging, lunges less and wins about half as many tackles as before.
- Closer ball control. Less slowdown when you carry the ball, and a short shield on your first touch after a pass.
- Crisper passes.
- The computer plays the same way: it uses through balls, runs and skill moves, so matches flow both ways.
- New touch buttons, Thru and Skill. How to play, the start screen hints and the game page list the new controls.
- Through ball and Skill move are in Settings, Change controls, so you can move them to any key or button.
- How to play is a full-screen panel that scrolls. Before, its text ran off the screen with Larger text. The stick or D-pad scrolls it.
- Music starts on the first press anywhere. Since v0.4.0 the start screen stayed quiet until you picked a slot.

## Controls

- Xbox controller: A pass, Y through ball, X skill move (tackle without the ball), B shoot or tackle, RT sprint, LB switch.
- Keyboard: J or Space pass, L through ball, I skill move, K or F shoot, Shift sprint, Tab switch.
- Touch: Pass, Thru, Skill, Shoot and Sprint on the right.

X no longer shoots. B shoots. Change controls can move any of these.

## Test checklist

- [ ] Each skill move with a controller, a keyboard and touch.
- [ ] A through ball to a runner, then a goal.
- [ ] Defense feels relaxed on Normal. Try Hard too.
- [ ] The Thru and Skill buttons sit well on your phone and do not cover the pitch.
- [ ] Music starts on the start screen after the first press.
- [ ] Change controls lists Through ball and Skill move, and a remap works in a match.
