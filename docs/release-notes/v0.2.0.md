# Lo-Fi League v0.2.0

League mode.

## New in this version

- League mode: pick a club and play a 7 matchday season against the other 7 clubs.
- The other three matches each matchday play out by themselves, based on each club's player ratings.
- Standings table with played, won, drawn, lost, goal difference and points. Win 3 points, draw 1.
- The table screen shows the next match and the last matchday's results.
- Your season saves after every match. Leave and come back later to carry on.
- At the end of a season you see the champions and your finish. Your best finish and titles are kept.
- Two new clubs: Lamplight Wanderers and Window Seat Albion. Eight clubs in all.
- A new mode screen after Start: Exhibition, League or Practice.
- The club screen has a Back button. Practice moved from a tick box to its own mode.
- Version code 3. Signed with the real key, so it installs over v0.1.1.

## Test checklist

1. On a phone with v0.1.1 installed: install v0.2.0 over it. No uninstall needed. If v0.1.0 is installed, uninstall it first.
2. Open the game. Press Start. The mode screen shows Exhibition, League and Practice.
3. Pick League. Pick a club. The table shows 8 clubs at 0 points and Matchday 1 of 7.
4. Press Play matchday 1. The match starts with a message naming the matchday.
5. Play to full time. The full time screen shows your place in the table. Press Back to league.
6. The table shows Matchday 1 results, including your match, and points for every club.
7. Press Back, then close the game. Open it again, press Start. The League button reads League: matchday 2 of 7.
8. Play all 7 matchdays. After the last one the table shows the champions, your finish and your best finish.
9. Press New season. Pick a club. A new season starts at 0 points.
10. Mid-season, press Start over twice. A new season starts. One press only changes the button text.
11. During a league match, open Menu and pick Quit to title. The match is not recorded. The same matchday waits for you.
12. Exhibition and Practice still work as in v0.1.

## Known limits

- Tested by a test bot (a full season and 6 matches), a rules check and headless Chrome. Not tested on a real phone, a controller or with speakers.
- Home and away do not change anything yet. Your club always attacks to the right.
- No cups, promotion or relegation yet.
