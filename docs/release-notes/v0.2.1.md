# Lo-Fi League v0.2.1

Save fixes and the shared kit. Gameplay is the same as v0.2.0.

## New in this version

- Your season saves right away. Before, closing or reloading the browser just after full time could lose that match.
- Your wins, draws, losses, seasons, titles and best finish now survive a reload. In v0.2.0 they went back to 0 each time the game started.
- The game also saves when the app goes to the background or closes.
- Touch controls hide when you press a key or use a controller. They come back on the next touch.
- Built on the shared kit 1.1.0 from `game-standards`: the save, the browser test bridge, signing and the tests.
- Version code 4. Signed with the real key. Installs over v0.1.1 and v0.2.0.

## Test checklist

1. On a phone with v0.2.0: install v0.2.1 over it. No uninstall needed.
2. Start a league season and play matchday 1 to full time. Press Back to league.
3. Close the game at once. Open it again. The League button reads League: matchday 2 of 7.
4. Finish an exhibition match. Close and reopen the game. Finish another one. The full time screen counts both results in your record.
5. In the browser: play a league match to full time, then reload the page at once. The season is still on matchday 2.
6. Touch the screen: the touch controls show. Press a key: they hide. Touch again: they show.
7. The game page shows one line under the buttons: Version 0.2.1. Release notes · Backlog · Source on GitHub.

## Known limits

- Tested by the rules check, a test bot (a full season and 6 matches), a browser test and an end-to-end test of two full seasons in headless Chrome. Not tested on a real phone, a controller or with speakers.
- v0.2.0 records that were already lost cannot come back. Records count again from this version.
- In a browser a save needs about half a second to reach storage. Closing the tab in that half second can lose the last match. A test browser showed the delay grows when the frame rate drops.
