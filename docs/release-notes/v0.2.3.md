# Lo-Fi League v0.2.3

Comfort release: bigger text on phones, a match that pauses itself and the goal scorers at full time. Gameplay is the same as v0.2.2.

## New in this version

- Bigger text on phones and tablets. Every panel, button and label is 25 percent larger on a touch screen. Text in the Menu switches between Large and Normal, and the game remembers your choice.
- A match pauses itself when the game loses focus: a phone call, another app or another browser tab. The Menu opens and the clock stops until you press Resume.
- Quit to title in a league match asks first. The button reads "Quit? It will not count". Press it again to quit. Restart match and quitting an exhibition work as before.
- The game remembers the last mode and club you played. Start, then A or Enter twice, takes you back to them.
- The full time screen lists who scored and in which minute, such as "TDR: Remy 2', Milo 5'".
- The Menu row wraps onto a second line when it is too wide for the screen, so no button hides under Close.
- Built on the shared kit 1.5.0 from `game-standards`.
- Version code 6. Signed with the real key. Installs over v0.1.1 and every v0.2 version. Your season and records stay.

## Test checklist

1. On a phone with v0.2.2: install v0.2.3 over it. Your league table and records are still there.
2. The title card text is larger than before. Open the Menu in a match: Text reads Large. Press it: everything shrinks to Normal. Press it again for Large.
3. Close the game and open it again. The text size you picked is still there.
4. Play a practice match with your second favourite club. Quit to title. Press Start: Practice has the highlight. Press A or Enter: your club has the highlight.
5. In a league match, open the Menu and press Quit to title once. It reads "Quit? It will not count". Press it again. The league table has not moved on.
6. In a match, switch to another app or take a call, then come back. The Menu is open and the clock has stopped. Press Resume.
7. Score a goal and play to full time. The full time card names the scorer and the minute.
8. The game page shows one line under the buttons: Version 0.2.3. Release notes · Backlog · Source on GitHub.

## Known limits

- Tested by the rules check, a test bot (a full season and 6 matches), a pretend Xbox controller test, a comfort test on a phone-size screen, a browser test and an end-to-end test of two full seasons in headless Chrome. Not tested on a real phone, with a real controller or with speakers.
- The pause on focus loss was tested by telling the browser page it lost focus. A real phone call or app switch was not tried.
- Minutes count real match time, 1 to 6, because a match is two 3-minute halves.
- On a desktop screen with Large text the Menu row takes two lines.
