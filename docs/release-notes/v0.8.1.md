# Lo-Fi League v0.8.1

Lo-Fi League catches up on the studio standards. You now have a player level, three daily goals and trophies for your season results. A new Locker holds the looks you unlock: balls, boots, super trail colors and a night pitch.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.8.0. Your saves, records and league stay.
2. In the browser, the game goes full screen on your first tap or click.
3. Open Menu, then Records. You see your level, today's three goals and the 8 trophies.
4. Play an exhibition. Goals, a win and good passes count toward today's goals. A note shows when one is done.
5. From the mode screen open the Locker. Press a row to switch to another look you own.

## New

- Player level (G4.20): every match, goal, win and super goal gives XP. Each level unlocks one look. Level 10 takes about two hours.
- Daily goals (G4.19): three a day from normal play, such as Score 5 goals, Win a match or Score a super goal. Finishing all three unlocks a look. A missed day costs nothing and there is no streak.
- Trophies for season results (G4.18): League Champions, Runners-up, Third Place, Top Half, Unbeaten Season, Goal Rush, Tight at the Back and one secret trophy. Records shows a percent complete.
- Locker: ball designs, boot colors for your club, super shot trail colors and the Night lights pitch.
- The slot list shows your level and trophies.

## Changed

- Full screen in the browser on the first tap, click or key press (G5.11). Turning it off in Settings is remembered.
- Up to 120 frames a second on fast screens (G6.20). The match steps every frame.
- Fills 1920 x 1080 and 2400 x 1080 screens edge to edge (G6.21).

## Judgment calls

1. The daily goals, trophy list and Locker looks are my picks (16 looks, 12 goals, 8 trophies), all in `data/progress.json`. Night lights is the last level unlock.
2. Locker boots color only your club. The computer side keeps black boots.
3. Goals and XP count only in your own exhibition and league matches, not in practice or the title card match.
4. The Locker sits on the mode screen as a fourth button. The Menu row is unchanged.
5. The rules test now accepts a goal after a throw-in, since arcade play can score within its 400 steps.
6. The slot line keeps the record first and adds the level and trophies after it.
