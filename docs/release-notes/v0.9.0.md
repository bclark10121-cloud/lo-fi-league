# Lo-Fi League v0.9.0

Animal teams. Six clubs now field a full squad of one animal each, with new motions, a goal replay and a closer camera you choose in Settings.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.8.2. Your saves stay.
2. Start an exhibition as Window Seat Albion. Your whole team is rabbits. The other side is its own animal, or people if you drew Midnight Vinyl FC or Slow Fade Athletic.
3. Pass and shoot. Each kick swings a leg at the ball. Stand still and the players breathe and shift, no frozen pose.
4. Score, or use Tester tools, Goal for you. You see the goal again in slow motion from a low camera, then your players cheer. Press A or tap to skip the replay.
5. Open the Menu while the players cheer. Every player holds still until you close it.
6. Open Settings. Camera starts on Medium. Try Wide and Close. The view moves in smoothly. Turn the phone upright and try each one again.

## New

- Animal athletes (picked by BC, 2026-11-21): Tape Deck Rovers are foxes, Rainy Window United frogs, Sunday Static cats, Cassette City raccoons, Lamplight Wanderers bears and Window Seat Albion rabbits. Keepers too. Each team wears its club colors, home, away and keeper.
- New motions: an idle stance, a real kick on every pass and shot, a slide on tackles, a stumble when knocked down and a victory cheer.
- Goal replay and celebrations (picked by BC): after each goal in your own matches, the last 3 seconds play again in slow motion from a low camera. Then the scorer and nearby teammates cheer. Skip with A or a tap. Settings has a Goal replay switch.
- Camera in Settings (asked for by BC, 2026-11-21): Wide, Medium and Close. Medium is the default (BC's pick). Wide is the camera you know. The closer views follow the ball more tightly and look ahead toward the goal you attack.

## Judgment calls

1. Kit speck rule kept at 3 of 8. The fresh review asked for a stricter rule so no fur beside a kit edge takes the club color. A builder tried 5 of 8. Kit pictures at 3, 4 and 5 showed that 5 brings back pink specks on the frog shirt and the fox sleeve, so 3 stays. A new test, `tests/speck_check.py`, guards it: at most 500 texels per texture may be painted more than 2 texels outside the kit. Measured: bear 15, cat 89, fox 397, frog 155, rabbit 19, raccoon 18, of about one million texels each.
2. The fox's shirt texture has a pink-tinted area the shader does not repaint (about 4,950 texels, one degree outside the shirt color window). It is hard to see at match size. A texture fix is in the backlog.
3. Motion clips: the stumble clip is trimmed to its 3.5 to 6.5 second part (the first 3.6 seconds are a still pose). The cheer is trimmed to 4 seconds.
4. The slide reads as a head-first belly slide. A feet-first clip would be better later.
5. The keeper dive keeps the old code tilt. The Meshy dive clip lands head first.
6. Heights: rabbit 3.0 yards, fox 2.6, raccoon 2.5, bear 2.45, cat 2.45, frog 2.3. Bears and frogs are wider than the rest.
7. Shirt numbers ride the upper spine bone. The fox's idle stance turns its upper body, so its number reads at an angle when it stands.
8. Rabbits look a little pink under the match light.
9. The GOAL banner overlaps the start of the replay. Own goals get no cheer.
10. The Camera choice also applies to the match behind the start screen.
11. Upright, Wide clips a net corner by about 7 px (the old camera, unchanged). Upright, every camera choice leaves the bottom of the screen as grass.
12. The browser build is 64.6 MB of the 70 MB budget (G6.16), up from 51 MB, because of the six animal models. Owls and turtles need a trim first. In the backlog.
13. The three game page pictures and the Game Shelf card picture were taken on 2026-11-21 from the v0.9.0 build, before the 2026-11-22 fixes. Those fixes do not change how a match looks.
14. The fresh review ran twice (G7.14): one review and one more look. Its last two findings were comment wording in the shader, fixed. Its notes are in the backlog.
