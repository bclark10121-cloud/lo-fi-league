# Lo-Fi League v0.1.0

First test build.

## New in this version

- 7 a side exhibition match against the computer. Two 3-minute halves.
- Six fictional clubs with home and away kits. The away kit shows when colors clash.
- Four formations stored as data.
- Pass, shoot with a power meter, sprint, tackle and switch player.
- Goals, kickoffs, throw-ins, corners and goal kicks. Posts and crossbar bounce the ball back.
- Computer keepers on both sides.
- Practice mode: no clock, only a keeper to beat.
- Four lo-fi tracks that play in turn. Crowd, whistle, kick and cheer sounds.
- Win, draw and loss record saved on the device.
- Keyboard, controller and touch controls. Menu button at the top right.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page. Tap Play in your browser. The title card shows over a computer match.
2. Press Start. Pick a club. The match starts with a kickoff. Lo-fi music plays.
3. Dribble right with D or the stick. Press Space to pass. Control moves to the player who receives it.
4. Hold F to fill the shot meter, then let go. Hold W or S while shooting to aim at a corner.
5. Hold Shift to sprint. The bar under the score drains and refills.
6. Lose the ball. Press Space to switch to the nearest player. Press F near the ball to tackle.
7. Put the ball over the sideline. A throw-in follows. On your throw-in, aim and press Space (short) or F (long).
8. Score a goal. A cheer plays and the match restarts from the center.
9. Wait for half time and full time. The final score, shots, possession and your record show.
10. Open Menu. Every option shows in a row. Sound and Music toggle. How to play opens. Esc closes it.
11. Tick Practice on the club screen. Only a keeper defends. The clock does not run.
12. On a phone: uninstall any older Lo-Fi League first. Install the APK. Drag on the left to move. Use Pass, Shoot and Sprint on the right.
13. With a controller: left stick moves, A passes or switches, X shoots or tackles, RT sprints, Back opens Menu.

## Known limits

- Tested by a test bot, a rules check and headless Chrome. Not tested on a real phone, a controller or with speakers.
- Headless Chrome ran the game slower than real time, so the throw-in path was checked by the rules check instead.
- The APK is signed with a test key. A later build signed with the real key needs this one uninstalled first.
- No offside, fouls, free kicks, penalties or substitutions yet.
- Throw-ins are taken with the feet in this build.
